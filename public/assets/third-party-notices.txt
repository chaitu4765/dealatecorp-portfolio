Platform icons: Simple Icons, https://simpleicons.org, CC0-1.0.
Icons identify the services offered and do not imply platform affiliation.
DC Creative Labs logo supplied by the site owner.

Services redesign patterns reviewed on 21st.dev:
Card Spotlight: https://21st.dev/aceternity/card-spotlight/default
Spotlight technique: https://news.21st.dev/blog/react-spotlight-effect-components
Shimmer Button: https://21st.dev/
Spotlight and shimmer effects independently implemented with native CSS and React.
Logo shader is an original procedural WebGL implementation.
Implementation comparison artwork generated for this project.

Capability flip cards: independently implemented React/CSS adaptation of the interaction reviewed at https://21st.dev/community/components/UmairXD/flip-card/default (Umair Waheed).

Molten Ring Carousel: component source supplied by the site owner. The WebGL
distance-field, fusion, strand and glass-refraction rendering is integrated in
src/components/ui/molten-ring-carousel.tsx, with brand navigation, contained
logos, theme styling, responsive sizing and an accessible fallback.

Scroll-locked hero: adapted from React source supplied by the site owner,
whose demo credits guglielmogiannattasio.exe (guglielmogiannattasio.it).
The scroll-scrubbing interaction is reworked as branded CSS doors revealing
the live homepage, with scroll release, modal keyboard controls, skip and
reduced-motion support. The demo's city video is not used.

Logo scroll specimen: adapted from the site-owner-supplied Lycoris Specimen
by Kedhareswer (https://21st.dev/@kedhareswer). The scene timing, scroll holds
and composition transitions are adapted for DealateCorp branding and content.
The demo flower, font specimen text, blur and grain are not used.
